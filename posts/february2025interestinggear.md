There is an affliction that strikes many guitarists, bassists, drummers, et al.  It fuels much consternation.  It's FOMO-esque.  It's one of the many activities we engage in that allows us to avoid actually, you know, making music.

Gear Acquisition Syndrome is serious.  But also rad.  So here's some gear I've been using recently

## Fender '68 Custom Deluxe Reverb

I'm a long time devotee of the '65 Twin Reverb Reissue.  Big.  Loud.  2x12".  85ish watts.  Clean.  Powerful.

And also heavy.  And beyond loud.  It just wasn't fitting my needs any longer.
